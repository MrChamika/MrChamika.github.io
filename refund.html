Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Our commitment: We want you to love your jacket. If something isn't right, we'll do our best to make it right within 7 days of delivery.

1. Return Window

You may request a return within 7 days of receiving your order. After this period, we are unable to accept returns unless the item is defective or was incorrectly sent.

2. Eligible Items for Return

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition as when you received it
  • With original tags attached
  • In the original packaging
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase (order ID or email confirmation)

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original tags or packaging
  • Sale or clearance items marked as final sale
  • Items returned after 7 days of delivery

4. Return Process

To initiate a return:

  • Contact us via our Contact page with your order ID and reason for return.
  • We will respond within 2 working days with instructions.
  • Ship the item to our returns address (provided in the reply email).
  • Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer, except in cases of defective or incorrect items.

5. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund within 3 working days.

  • Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5–10 working days.
  • Rejected refunds (item not in original condition) will be returned to you at your cost.

6. Exchanges

We offer exchanges for a different size or colour of the same product, subject to availability. Contact us within 7 days of delivery to arrange an exchange. Exchange shipping fees may apply.

7. Defective or Incorrect Items

If you received a defective or wrong item, please contact us immediately with photos of the item. In such cases, we will arrange a free return and issue a full refund or replacement at no extra cost to you.

8. Contact

For return and refund requests, please contact us through our Contact page. Include your order number in the message.
