Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or create an account on Italian Tailors, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Shipping address (street, city, postal code)
  • Order history and product preferences
  • Device information and browsing activity on our site (via Firebase Analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfil your orders
  • Send order confirmation and shipping notifications
  • Maintain and improve our website
  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Prevent fraud and ensure security

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), which complies with international data protection standards. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Payment Information

We do not store your card details. All payment transactions are processed securely by PayHere, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. Please refer to PayHere's privacy policy for details on how they handle your payment information.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart session and site preferences. We do not use advertising cookies or sell cookie data to third parties.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our contact page.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
